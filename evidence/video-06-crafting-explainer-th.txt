SYSTEMS
[RESOLVED] The "Level 1 Upgrade Meta" Explained:

The player explicitly confirms via voiceover exactly how and why this works.

If you apply a Trait (Purple Gems) or a Rank (Blue Gems/Cash) to a card at Level 50, the game charges you an astronomical fee scaled to that card's Level 50 base value.

If you apply the Trait/Rank at Level 1, the cost is calculated based on its Level 1 base value, which is thousands of times cheaper.

The Meta Loop: Pull a top-tier card -> KEEP IT AT LEVEL 1 -> Use the Trait Machine until you roll "Almighty" or "Rich" -> Use the Rank Machine until you hit "UR" -> Then use Cash to level the card up to 50.

[RESOLVED] Card Crafting Rules:

When crafting Evolutions, the game completely destroys the component cards.

The game does not care if the component cards are Level 1 or Level 50. It does not transfer the levels to the new card. The resulting Evolved card will always pop out at Level 1.

Therefore, players are explicitly warned: Never level up a card you intend to use as crafting fodder. It is a complete waste of cash.

[RESOLVED] Automation Paywall:

The "Conveyor Settings" UI that allows you to auto-filter and auto-buy packs based on mutations is explicitly tied to the Auto Conveyor Gamepass (649 Robux). Free-to-play players must manually click to buy packs and cannot automatically filter out bad mutations.

[UPDATED] Infinity Tower "Equip Best" Trap:

When a player clicks "Equip Best" to optimize their Tower/Raid team, the game physically pulls those cards off the pedestals in the base and locks them into the combat team.

If a player wants to craft with a card that was auto-equipped to the Tower, they must manually enter the Tower UI, remove the card from the combat slots, and bring it back to their inventory before the Crafter NPC will recognize it.
