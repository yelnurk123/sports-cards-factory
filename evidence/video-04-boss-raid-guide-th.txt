SYSTEMS
[NEW] Combat Stats (HP & DMG): Cards do not display their combat stats on the normal base UI (which only shows Cash/sec). Combat stats must be checked by inserting the card into the Infinity Tower.

[NEW] The "Level 1 Upgrade" Meta: A critical gameplay mechanic: The cost of using the Rank Machine (Blue gems/Cash) and the Trait Machine (Purple gems) scales dynamically based on the card's current Level. Players optimally roll for Ranks and Traits while a card is Level 1 to save massive amounts of currency, and only level the card up to 50 with Cash after the desired Rank/Trait is secured.

[NEW] Artifact System: Boss Raids drop physical Artifact items (e.g., Infinity Blindfold, Divine Wheel). Players place these in their base to gain permanent global buffs to Cash, Raid Drop Luck, General Luck, and Mutation Luck. Artifacts can be leveled up (using Raid Shards) to increase their buff percentages. Players cannot stack the exact same level artifact for infinite buffs; there are diminishing or non-stacking returns.

[NEW] Traits System: The Trait Machine uses Purple Gems to assign a permanent modifier to a specific card. Traits drastically alter the card's utility (e.g., "Rich" doubles its Cash generation on the conveyor belt, "Tank" gives it +75% HP for Boss Raids/Tower, "Almighty" likely massively buffs all stats).
