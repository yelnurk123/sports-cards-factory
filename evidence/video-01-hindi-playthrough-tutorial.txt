*Mechanics:*

* **Packs/Spawners:** You buy a pack at the front of your base. It goes to your inventory or you immediately place it on an empty pedestal. Wait a few seconds to open.
* **Cards:** When opened, the pack reveals a Card. Cards have a Base Value (e.g., $15/Card). They generate boxes on the conveyor belt.
* **Conveyor:** Boxes move down the belt. You walk up, click "Carry [Value]", run to the "Sell Card Boxes!" bin, and deposit them for Cash.
* **Upgrades:** Click a placed card to upgrade it. Costs cash. Increases the value of the boxes it generates.
* **Rarities/Types:** Normal, Golden, Diamond, Venomous, Rainbow, Sakura, Candy, Blessed, Radioactive, Glitch, etc.
* **Card Tiers:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret.
* **Packs seen:** Ice Pack ($100), Sand Pack ($250), Fire Fighter/Inferno Pack ($100K), Lightning Pack ($1M), Dark Pack ($100M).

*UI Elements:*

* **Top Center:** Cash indicator (e.g., $54.8K).
* **Left Bar:** Shop, Index, Upgrade, Items, Ready! (PlayTime Rewards).
* **Right Bar:** 2x Offline Cash, 2x Luck, 2x Cash, VIP, Luck 1.00x, Cash 1.00x.
* **Top Bar (later):** Plaza, Base, Sell. (Teleports).

*Extraction: Systems*

* **Core Economy:** Packs -> Cards -> Boxes -> Sell -> Cash -> Better Packs/Upgrades.
* **Pedestal System:** Base has a limited number of pedestals (starts at around 10). Player must buy Base Expansions to get more. #### SCREENS

* **Main HUD:** Left vertical bar (`Shop, Index, Upgrade, Items, Ready!`). Right vertical bar (`Boosts, VIP`). Top center (`Cash`). Top bar (`Teleports: Plaza, Base, Sell`).
* **Index:** Accessed via left UI. Left column lists variant types (Normal, Golden, Diamond, Venomous, Rainbow, Sakura, Candy, Blessed, Radioactive, Glitch, Starfallen, Admin, Unknown). Main area shows grid of undiscovered cards with padlocks. Progress bar at top.
* **Shop:** Standard Roblox scrollable window. Contains Passes, Auto-features (Auto-Conveyor, Auto-Spawn, Auto-Buy), and Robux money packs.
* **PlayTime Rewards:** Grid showing rewards unlocking over session time (e.g., 5m, 10m, 30m). Yields Cash, Packs, and specific Cards (e.g., "Fire Fighter", "Cyber Runner").
* **Traits Machine / Roll (17:11):** Located in Plaza. Roll traits for cards using Gems.
* **Card Crafting (17:31):** Located in Plaza. Combine specific cards to make evolved/transcendent cards. E.g., Rengoku requires Wind Slayer, Serpent Slayer, Fog Slayer + $1.8M.
* **Upgrades Menu:** Accessed via left UI "Upgrade". Contains permanent progression boosts (Base Expansion, Luck, Cash, Time, Speed) purchased with Cash.

#### SYSTEMS

* **Core Economic Loop:** Buy Packs -> Place on Pedestal -> Wait to Open -> Card generates Boxes on Conveyor -> Player Carries Box to Sell Zone -> Earn Cash -> Buy Better Packs/Upgrades.
* **Pedestal & Storage System:** The player's base has a limited number of empty pedestals (starts at 10). If full, the player must "Remove" a card to place a new pack. Removing a card puts it in Storage (accessed via 'Items' menu, capacity max 150). Cards in storage can be bulk-sold for cash at the Plaza NPC.
* **Pack Timers:** Packs require real-world time to open once placed. Ice Packs take 5 seconds. Later packs take significantly longer (Lightning = 5 mins, Dark = 10 mins).
* **Card Anatomy:** A spawned Card has four main identifiers:
1. *Character/Name* (e.g., "Ice Princess")
2. *Base Value* (e.g., "$15/Card")
3. *Tier* (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret)
4. *Variant/Aura* (Normal, Golden, Diamond, Sakura, Venomous, etc.)


* **Card Upgrades:** Players can tap a placed card to pay Cash to level it up. Leveling up increases the card's base generation value per box.
* **Conveyor Generation:** The value of a box moving down the conveyor belt is the combined total of all active cards placed on the pedestals.
